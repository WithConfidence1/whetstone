## Interview trace

Six questions, every one answered in a few words or with a correction. What the interview surfaced that the raw ask did not contain:

- The Point: not a build, not a survey, but a first-principles charter born from a co-creation session, survey folded in as fuel.
- Ground Off: Cursor demoted from model to existence proof; "if the AI does the writing, we failed" added as a fence post.
- The Steel: Taylor's theory of writing (audience, ease, interest, epiphany; only the payload changes across jury, judge, YouTube); the wound (context-blind tools, copy-paste smuggling); the stance (me + AI as a third thing; attention spent on what it should be, never plumbing); source-blindness (ideas weighed only by ceiling, never origin).
- The Test Cut took three rounds. Taylor rejected a voice-ownership test (wrong frame), rejected a provenance test (source-blindness), and struck a feature-rejection test he found unhelpful. Final cut: the attention test and the ceiling test, nothing else.

Mid-run, Taylor also stress-tested the skill itself (does the whetstone metaphor lower its potential?). The interview held: metaphor stayed in the naming, plain definitions did the work, and his corrections reshaped the output rather than being absorbed politely.

## Compiled prompt (as read back)

The full compiled prompt is the Charter Session prompt: objective (co-creation session ending in a first-principles charter for an AI-first writing platform, landscape survey inside, beachhead question settled in-session), context (the eight steel points above), do not (no building, no generic essay, no feature list, no Cursor paradigms, no AI-does-the-writing, survey never votes), done means (attention test, ceiling test), deliver as (live session, then one markdown charter, saved where Taylor directs).

## Score and outcome

Edge reached 10/10 in six questions. Taylor fired the prompt; the charter session ran and the charter was ratified the same day (2026-08-30, filed in his vault under 95_Artifacts), with a design brief as the named next step. Skill fixes required by this run: none. Watch item for future runs: the Test Cut needed three rounds; the question bank's judging questions could probe for the user's own success theory earlier.
